MULTIPLAYER

      
Play with a friend on another phone or computer

        
Send your friend this game (the link or the file). One of you taps Create game (type your own code first if you like, for example SAKTHI) and tells the other the code. The other types it and taps Join. Then start any 3D game and your friends get an invite to join you. Works in normal Chrome with internet.

Online now

      
Everyone who has this game open at the same time shares the town: you see each other walk, drive, fly and dance, and you can chat. Open Phone, then Friends, to duel a friend, race them through the streets, ride in their car or go straight to them. In mini games you see your friends' live scores, and in Traffic Rider their bikes on the road. Up to 4 players can join one code. Your progress stays saved on your own device.

Sensei Kage's dojo
Shadow Duel
One-on-one silhouette duels, best of three rounds. Punch, kick, strike with your weapon, block, and unleash magic when your energy bar fills. Beat all six duelists to win the tournament.

      

      

      
Keyboard: A/D move (double-tap to dash) · W jump · J punch · K kick · L weapon · hold S to block · U magic. Press punch or weapon again mid-attack to chain a combo. On touch, double-tap an arrow to dash.
